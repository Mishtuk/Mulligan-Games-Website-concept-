      cEDH at Mulligan

      A monthly Championship Series, 5K weekends and a November invitational. Forty to 120 players an event, and a dual land on the line every month.

      Prizes
      Every Championship Series guarantees a dual land. Each winner gets an invitational seat.
      Proxies
      Up to 10, judge-approved, printed with full rules text.
      Entry
      Championship Series $60, 5K $100, Redemption $35 ($25 after the 5K). A $10 online deposit holds a spot; the rest is paid at the door.
      Parking
      Any lot next to the shop except the RISE lot.

              Good to know

              Can kids come?

              Yes. Learn to Play Pokemon on Wednesdays at 7 PM is a good first night.

              Do I need to sign up?

              Most weekly nights are walk-in. Drafts and Pauper have a seat limit, so sign up here to hold a seat. Pay at the store.

              Can I just come and play?

              Yes. Free play all day, every day we're open.

              How do preorders work?

              In store or by phone. Each preorder has a last day, shown above.
