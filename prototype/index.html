          Good to know

          Can kids come?
          Yes. Learn to Play Pokemon on Wednesdays at 7 PM is a good first night.
          Do I need to sign up?
          Most weekly nights are walk-in. Drafts and Pauper have a seat limit, so sign up here to hold a seat. Pay at the store.
          Can I just come and play?
          Yes. Free play all day, every day we're open.
          How do preorders work?
          In store or by phone. Each preorder has a last day, shown above.
