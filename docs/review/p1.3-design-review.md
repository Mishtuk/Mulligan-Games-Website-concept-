- **Player:** it reads like the shop's flyer come alive: red field, the week dealt as cards, "Open till midnight
  tonight" first, then tonight's tables. Card states are clear at a glance (seats, full, cancelled stamp, on now). The
  cEDH band gives the store its identity. It feels like a place to go on a Friday, not a template.

  payment is one tap, walk-ins sit in a sticky bar above the tab bar, and Undo replaces confirms. With the plain-word
  event buttons and the visible menu icon it's fast. Phone works but is a fallback (P1 above).
