## Top takeaways for the site
1. **Events are the product.** About 20 events are listed in 9 days: daily open play, FNM draft, Standard Showdown, Pauper, Pre-Modern, Magic Academy. The home page should be a this-week schedule by day, filterable by game/format, with fee and cap on every card.
2. **cEDH is the brand.** Mulligans Championship Series (monthly, 40-80 players, guaranteed dual-land prizes) plus 5K cEDH tournaments (73-120 players) and Redemption events. Give cEDH its own section: circuit points, invites, standings, proxy rule (10 max, judge-approved), results archive.
3. **Fix the address.** Sources conflict: 445 York Rd (Apple Maps, TopDeck, KeepUpCards, AllEvents) vs 455 York Rd (Wizards locator, our brief). Confirm with the owners before publishing; the site should become the canonical source. Also publish phone, hours, and the parking note (do not park in the adjacent RISE lot).
4. **Hours are late-night and public listings may be stale.** Wed-Thu 2-10pm, Fri-Sat 2pm-12am, Sun 1-10pm, Mon-Tue closed (Apple Maps / KeepUpCards). The Wizards page shows none. Admin should edit hours and holiday overrides once and show them everywhere.
5. **They already use many external tools** (Wizards Locator/EventLink, TopDeck.gg, Spicerack, AllEvents, Discord, Patreon). Don't replace them. Create an event once, then output links/embeds to each (TopDeck for cEDH, Wizards for FNM) plus .ics.
6. **Their info is scattered.** Events live on Wizards, TopDeck, Spicerack, AllEvents and Facebook, and listings disagree (KeepUpCards says "Pokemon only"). One site that aggregates them is the real value.
7. **Retail side matters.** Singles (MTG, Pokemon), sealed, trade-ins, play space. Reviewers praise selection and community; one complains about prices at or above market. Show preorders (Riftbound, One Piece, Hololive OCG coming) and a buylist/trade-in page.
8. **Charity and anniversary events** (CHOP cEDH charity, Anniversary Weekend) deserve highlight slots.
9. **Capacity matters.** Caps of 28 (Pauper, FNM) and 16 (Saturday draft) exist, so sign-up with waitlist is directly useful.
