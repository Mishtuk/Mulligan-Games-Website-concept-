## Top takeaways for Mulligan Games (for the designer and coder)
1. **The weekly schedule is the product.** The store posts one flyer per weekday (Wed to Sun) plus a master "Weekly Events" image (featured, posted 2026-04-21). The home page should be that schedule as live data ("Today / This week", filter by game). Everything else (prereleases etc.) is a one-off layered on top.
2. **Sign-up is fragmented.** Prerelease and anniversary events: "pre-registration in store only". Riftbound Summoner Skirmish: a tinyurl link (tinyurl.com/RBMulliganGames). Hololive Bloom Cup: pre-register on Bushiroad. Weekly nights: just show up. A site with a per-event "How to sign up" plus online capacity/waitlist and "pay in store" is a direct upgrade.
3. **Capacity and minimums matter.** Flyers state caps and minimums ("32 player cap", "8 players needed to fire", "Cap 50", D&D cap 5, painting lessons capacity 6). Admin needs capacity, min-to-fire, entry fee, prize text.
4. **Prize language is simple:** "prizing paid based on record", "store credit payout based on participation", raffles, promos, playmat to winner. Model prizes as free text plus optional store credit.
5. **Event shapes to support:** weekly recurring; "Nth weekday of the month" (2nd Saturday Riftbound, 1st Sunday cEDH); multi-date series (Standard RCQ on 3 dates); prerelease blocks (2 to 3 days, several time slots, one fee); multi-day weekend with sub-events (Anniversary); charity events.
6. **Last-minute changes are common** (Hobbit prerelease Sat slot cancelled; precon length corrected 120 to 80 min on Oct 14). Show an "Updated" badge.
7. **Preorders are a steady post type** ("Preorders only available in-store", with an end date: Delta Reign and Star Trek end Nov 1). Preorder tracker with deadline fits.
8. **Community proof posts work:** turnout counts and winners ("69 players competing for one Volcanic Island", "52 planeswalkers"), monthly cEDH winner gets a November invitational seat, food drives and charity (CHOP, Warminster Food Bank). Give the site a results/winners feed.
9. **Customers ask basic questions in comments:** "Do you allow children for the Learn Pokemon / Saturday play? 9 year old." (staff: yes), "How much does it cost for pre-order?", "Is there a prerelease today?", "Where are Gundam cards?". Put age policy, price and "is it happening today" on each event page. One customer called a $175 Pokemon ETB "predatory" (Sep 16 post): price transparency matters.
10. **Hours are not on the page** (only "Closed now"). The site must state hours (ask the owner). Flyer says "Closed Monday & Tuesday" and "Free Play All Day, Everyday".

## How events are announced today
- **Flyer images**, designed in-house: red template with a white diamond and a black "Free Play All Day Everyday!" circle, one per weekday; prerelease flyers use set art and "Preregistration available in store only, Entry $X". Prizes, caps and times are in the image only, not in post text, so they are not searchable.
- **Very short post text** ("Pre-orders available also!", "Shine bright with Riftbound Radiance! Available today!").
- **Facebook Events** only for the biggest events (Anniversary, CHOP charity, 2025 prereleases); not used for weekly nights.
- **Sign-up** is mostly in store; a few use external tools (tinyurl form, Bushiroad).
- Changes are made by new posts or edited text, not new flyers.
