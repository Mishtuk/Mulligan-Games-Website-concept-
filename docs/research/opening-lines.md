# Opening lines: what card shops say, and what Mulligan should say

Researcher: Mulligan Games team. Checked 2026-10-27. Extends `stores-and-sites.md` (not repeated here).

## Recommendation

**Headline: "Pull up a chair."**
**Sub-line: "Free play every day we're open. Tonight we're here until midnight, and there's a seat for you."**
(Sub-line is 20 words. Swap the tail for the live status: "Closed today, back Wednesday at 2 PM.")

Why: it says the one thing that is true of Mulligan and rare on store sites (free play, big room, friendly staff) in a
voice a shop owner would actually speak, with no "ultimate", "gateway" or "your turn" wordplay. It also works for all
games (Magic, Pokemon, Warhammer, D&D) and survives being closed, because it is an invitation, not a status.

**Status chip (replaces the old headline's job), placed above the headline or beside the hours:**
- Open: `Open now · till midnight`
- Before opening: `Opens 2 PM today`
- Closing soon (last 60 min): `Open · closing at midnight`
- Closed for the day: `Closed · back Wed 2 PM`
- Holiday/special: `Closed Thanksgiving · back Fri 12 PM`
Green dot when open, neutral when closed. The chip is generated from the hours data, never hand-typed.
(Opening times above are placeholders; use the real hours from the store data.)

## Six headline options

| # | Headline (words) | Sub-line (words) | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 **(rec.)** | Pull up a chair. (4) | Free play every day we're open. Tonight we're here until midnight, and there's a seat for you. (19) | Host at the door |
| 2 | Your seat's open. (3) | Big tables, free play, friendly staff. Open till midnight tonight, whatever you're playing. (14) | Community / "your table" |
| 3 | Come play. We saved you a table. (6) | Magic, Pokemon, One Piece, Warhammer and more. Free play all day, open till midnight tonight. (15) | Warm, plain |
| 4 | Warminster's table for everyone. (4) | Free play every day in a big, friendly room on York Road. Open till midnight tonight. (16) | Place-based |
| 5 | Shuffle up. Everyone's welcome. (4) | New to the game or twenty years in, there's a table here. Open till midnight tonight. (16) | Card-shop jargon, welcoming |
| 6 | Good games. Good people. Good food. (6) | Free play, trade nights and food drives, right here on York Road. Open until midnight tonight. (16) | Matches "Food and Fellowship" |

Notes on each (judgement):
1. "Pull up a chair" is a phrase people say to guests; closest to how staff talk. Not found on any store I checked.
2. Slightly more "your table" (a pattern the big stores use); a little soft for a cEDH crowd.
3. Names the games in the sub-line, so the headline can stay human. "Saved you a table" is a hair cute.
4. Most place-based; good for search/local trust, but "for everyone" borders on a stock phrase.
5. "Shuffle up" is insider talk that signals a real card shop; the "everyone's welcome" half is common.
6. Strongest tie to the real trade-night and food-bank identity; three-beat lines are close to cliche, and it
   promises food, so confirm there is food on offer on ordinary nights.

Avoid: "Your turn" (Red Castle), "Eat. Drink. Play." (Mox), "Your home for tabletop gaming" (Guardian), "Welcome to X"
(Pandemonium), "Building community through the power of games" (Wizard's Keep), "friendly and inclusive" (Gamelandia).

## Patterns across stores (what I saw)

- **Retail giants (Card Kingdom, CoolStuffInc, Hareruya, Uncle's, F2F) lead with product or an event, not a welcome.**
  Their "headline" is a product category or a promo ("Singles", "CoolStuffCon ... is free to attend!"). That's a
  webstore voice. Mulligan's front page is a room, not a webstore.
- **Physical, play-first stores lead with people and place.** Mox ("Eat. Drink. Play."), Guardian ("Your home for tabletop
  gaming!"), Pandemonium ("Welcome to Pandemonium / We're glad you're here"), Wizard's Keep ("Building community..."),
  Gamelandia ("friendly and inclusive"), Game Kastle ("Impressive Gaming Spaces, Daily Scheduled Events" per the
  earlier research).
- **Recurring words:** home, community, welcome, play, friendly/inclusive. Almost nobody uses "table" or "seat" or
  "chair" as the headline, which leaves a gap for a hosting voice.
- **Almost none state tonight's hours as the headline.** Hours live in the header/footer or a strip. Hence the chip.
- **Buttons:** welcome-style heroes point to "Visit [city]" (Guardian), "Who We Are" (Pandemonium), "LOCATIONS"
  (Dreamers Vault); product heroes say "SHOP". Mulligan's first button should be "See this week's events" with
  "Get directions" second.
- Short beats long: every real tagline here is under 8 words.
