# Mulligan Games: photos, logo and clips from the store's own accounts

Collected 2026-10-26 (owner approved use). Logged out, public pages only; no login bypassed. Originals live in git-ignored `source/social/`; web copies are in `prototype/`.

## Summary
Logged-out pages expose only a handful of images (Instagram ~12 grid thumbs at max 640px, Facebook ~5, TikTok avatar, X avatar). Genuine shop photos found: 4. The rest are the store's own flyers. Instagram, Facebook photos tab and TikTok full grids need scrolling or login for more; Google Maps, TopDeck and AllEvents gave no store images (AllEvents returned a generic stock page; TopDeck has no public store page found). X posts are behind a login wall.

## Logo (no vector or hi-res original is public)
The IG, TikTok and X avatars are photos of the rooftop sign (150 / 398 / 329 px); the Facebook avatar is a photo of the brick-wall sign (720px). The best flat artwork is the wordmark on the store's own flyers (same stylised M, MULLIGAN over GAMES, blue with purple outline). Recommended file: `prototype/img/logo/logo-native-340.png` (crisp, 340x120) or `logo-512.png` for a square tile; ask the owner for the original file for a hero-size logo.
Colours (sampled, approx): fill #70C0E0, outline #581060, inner accent ~#F0905C; real sign blue ~#2F5FB0 with red outline.
