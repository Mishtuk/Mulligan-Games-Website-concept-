## How the site should use this (calm, not blasting)
- **One photo per section**, never a wall: hero = `shop-floor-cases`; This week = `event-full-room`; cEDH page =
  `event-cedh-tables` or `champion-belt`; Warhammer/tabletop = `warhammer-table`; New here = `play-room-empty`;
  Visit = `shop-floor-cases-800` or `singles-case-lit`.
- **Reviews as one quiet row**: "4.9 on Google, 81 reviews" + 3 one-line quotes + "Read reviews on Google". No carousel.
- **Community facts as a short list**, not flyers: the food drive trade nights, the yearly anniversary weekend, the
  painting contest. Flyers stay on social media.
- Prefer wide shots where customers are small; close portraits (winners, staff) only where the store already posted
  them proudly (winner photos at the door banner).
