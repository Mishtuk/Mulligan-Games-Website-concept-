# Mulligan Games: store's own videos (front-page video candidates)

Checked 2026-10-26, view only (no login, no downloads). Limits: TikTok/Instagram grids load only partly while logged out and I could not read per-video dates/lengths for most; X posts are hidden behind a login wall (not viewed). Judgement is marked as such.

## Top takeaways
1. **Nothing in the archive is a ready-made "store loop".** ~90% of the store's short video is hand-held close-ups of graded Pokemon cards (PSA/CGC slabs) over the glass case, with on-screen text and trending audio. Good for product posts, poor for a hero loop. Audio is mostly licensed TikTok music: do not reuse sound on the site.
2. **Best candidates (judgement):**
   - Facebook "We have finally moved to our new location..." (0:22, ~2 years ago, 1.6K views, 57 reactions): shows the building/lot at 445 York Rd. Most "place" footage found; recognisable storefront. Check the audio and whether people appear before use.
   - Instagram reel DTHcsxqjNDL (Jan 4, 2026, collab with @perky_grn_fairy): a cEDH tournament win; opens on a group of players seated at the tables. Best "community/events" feel, but identifiable adults and it is the customer's post (needs their OK).
   - Instagram "Event Added - Mulligan Games Warminster, PA" reel (text graphic, no people): usable as a style reference or events teaser, not a loop.
3. **Recommendation:** shoot a fresh 10-15s vertical-safe horizontal clip (door, shelves, empty play tables, case lights), no faces, no audio. The existing archive can supply stills and event-graphic ideas, not the loop.
4. **Privacy:** Only one video (Jan 2026 IG reel) clearly shows identifiable customers (5+ adults at tables). No children seen in anything I viewed, but the grids were only partly viewable and Pokemon event footage may exist further down: re-check before publishing any clip. Do not embed from social platforms without the owner's own re-export.
